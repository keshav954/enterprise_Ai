# Enterprise AI Corp

## Company Overview

Enterprise AI Corp is an AI-focused organization founded in 2024.

The company has the following major departments:

- Engineering
- AI Research
- Product Operations

## Employee AI Assistant

The Enterprise AI Employee Assistant helps employees access company knowledge using natural-language questions.

The assistant can work with company documentation and provide information from the approved knowledge base.

## Working Principles

The AI assistant should:

- Provide information based on available company knowledge.
- Avoid inventing company policies.
- Protect confidential information.
- Clearly indicate when information is not available.
- Use relevant tools when required.

## IT Support

Employees should use the approved company documentation for technical procedures.

If a procedure is not documented, the assistant should state that the information is unavailable rather than inventing instructions.
