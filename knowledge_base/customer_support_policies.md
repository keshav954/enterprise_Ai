# Customer Support Policy & Escalation Framework

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines Enterprise AI Corp's official customer support commitments, SLA tiers, refund guidelines, and complaint resolution procedures.

## 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Tiers
Support inquiries are classified according to business severity:
- **P1 - Critical Outage / Data Loss:** Response time within **1 hour**. Resolution target within **6 hours**. Direct escalation to Engineering Lead.
- **P2 - Major Feature Impairment:** Response time within **4 hours**. Resolution target within **24 hours**. Escalation to Tier 2 Technical Support.
- **P3 - General Inquiries & Account Questions:** Response time within **1 business day** (24 hours). Resolved by Tier 1 Support.
- **P4 - Feature Requests & Feedback:** Response within **3 business days**. Logged into Product Backlog.

## 3. Return & Refund Guidelines
1. **Software Subscriptions:**
   - Full 100% money-back guarantee within **30 days** of initial subscription purchase with no questions asked.
   - Pro-rated refund available if service experienced verified downtime exceeding SLA limits (99.9% uptime commitment).
2. **Hardware Products (e.g., Security Sentinel, Edge Gateway X1):**
   - 30-day return window for unopened hardware in original packaging (subject to 10% restocking fee).
   - Defective hardware qualifies for expedited advance replacement within **14 days** of delivery at zero customer cost.
3. **Professional Services & Consulting:**
   - Consulting hours already rendered are non-refundable. Unused prepaid consulting hours remain valid for 12 months.

## 4. Delay & Inconvenience Compensation
If an enterprise shipment or service provision is delayed beyond the agreed fulfillment date:
- **Delay of 3–7 business days:** Grant a complimentary 10% invoice discount on the next billing cycle.
- **Delay exceeding 7 business days:** Grant a 15% discount credit plus complimentary upgrade to Premium Support tier for 3 months.
- Customer Success Managers hold discretionary authority to issue service credits up to \$1,000 per incident.

## 5. Complaint Resolution Protocol
When responding to dissatisfied or upset enterprise clients:
1. **Acknowledge and Empathize:** Validate the customer's frustration immediately without defensive language.
2. **Present Facts & Timeline:** State the verified delivery or technical status transparently.
3. **Offer Actionable Remedy:** Provide immediate solutions (e.g., replacement tracking number, service credit, or direct engineer contact).
4. **Follow-Through Commitment:** Specify exact date and time of the next status check-in.
